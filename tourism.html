Agro Tourism

Gavathi Shetmal is an initiative by Rasai Agro, Harpavade, led by Mr. Nitin Shivaji Malekar. We are dedicated to bringing authentic, farm-fresh produce directly from the fertile lands of Ajara, Bhudargad, Chandgad, and Gadhinglaj (Kolhapur) to your doorstep in Mumbai. Our mission is to bridge the gap between hardworking farmers and health-conscious urban consumers by providing 100% natural and high-quality vegetables, fruits, and grains.
